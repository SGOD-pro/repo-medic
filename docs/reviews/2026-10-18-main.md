## Summary
This change extracts and unifies child process termination into a dedicated process control helper, resolving previously identified risks of hanging tests and uncollected timers during acceptance runs. The helper guarantees termination through SIGTERM with fallback escalation to SIGKILL, cleans up event listeners and timers, and verifies actual exit before resolving. The implementation is accompanied by thorough unit and regression tests covering stubborn processes, prompt termination, idempotency, hard ceiling timeouts, ESRCH immediate resolution, and detached process group termination.

## Minor
### 🟡 Missing hard ceiling timeout after SIGKILL escalation, `frontend/tests/process_control.mjs:56` [Resolved]
**Problem**: After escalating to SIGKILL when the timeout expires, the helper relies indefinitely on the exit event listener without a secondary timeout. If a process enters an uninterruptible sleep state or fails to terminate due to permission restrictions, the promise will hang indefinitely.
**Why it matters**: The helper was introduced specifically to prevent CI pipelines from hanging indefinitely. While SIGKILL reliably terminates virtually all userland processes, an unkillable process or missed signal delivery could still stall automated test runs.
**Suggested fix**: Add a secondary safety timeout of one to two seconds after sending SIGKILL that rejects the promise or warns and resolves if the process has still not exited.
**Resolution**: Added a configurable secondary hard ceiling timeout in stopProcess defaulting to 2000ms. If a process fails to exit after SIGKILL and the hard ceiling expires, the helper logs a warning, cleans up listeners, and resolves cleanly rather than hanging indefinitely.
